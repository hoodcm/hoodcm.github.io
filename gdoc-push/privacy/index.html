gdoc-push privacy policy

For gdoc-push, a personal tool by Michael Hood

What it reads

gdoc-push asks Google for access to Google Docs and Google Drive for the account that signs in. It reads only the Docs that account asks it to open: their text, their suggestions, and their comment threads.

What it writes

It adds suggested edits to those Docs, as the signed-in account. It never deletes a Doc or changes who it's shared with.

Where the data stays

The sign-in token and the copies of each Doc it pulls stay on the signed-in person's own computers. gdoc-push runs no server, sells nothing, shows no ads, and shares no data with anyone. Its use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

To withdraw access, remove gdoc-push at Google Account third-party access.
