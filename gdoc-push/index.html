gdoc-push

A personal tool by Michael Hood

gdoc-push is the Google sign-in behind folio, the private manuscript tooling Michael uses for his own papers. With it, folio pulls the text and comments of a Google Doc into a working copy and offers edits back to that Doc as suggestions, which the Doc's owner accepts or rejects in Google Docs.

It isn't offered to anyone else. It runs only on Michael's own computers, for the Google account he signs in with.

For what it reads and keeps, see the gdoc-push privacy policy.
